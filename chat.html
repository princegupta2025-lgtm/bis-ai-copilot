7. PERFORMANCE REQUIREMENTS

7.1 General — Helmets shall satisfy requirements for shock absorption, retention, and field of vision.

7.4 Impact Attenuation Test:
Helmets shall be subjected to impact attenuation test on a drop-tower anvil. Peak acceleration imparted to the headform shall not exceed 300 g (2 940 m/s²), and cumulative time during which acceleration exceeds 150 g shall not exceed 5.0 ms.

8.2 Retention System — Under a quasi-static dynamic load of 1.0 kN, dynamic displacement shall not exceed 35 mm, and residual displacement after load removal shall not exceed 25 mm.
