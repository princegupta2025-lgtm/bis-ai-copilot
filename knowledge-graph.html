Electric Kettle (Heating Appliances)

Domestic electric kettles require mandatory Scheme-I ISI certification. Key compliance links connect to IS 302 (Part 2/Sec 15):2024, dual dry-boil thermal protection (Clause 19.4), and NABL dielectric strength lab testing.
